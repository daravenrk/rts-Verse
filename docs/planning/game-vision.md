# Game Vision

Project identity: daravenrk's Command and Conquer meets Total Annihilation.

## Vision Statement

Build a fast and readable RTS with strong faction personality, satisfying base growth, and large-scale battlefield momentum while preserving modern usability and smooth control.

## Experience Pillars

- Aggressive early game pressure with clear tactical counters.
- Scalable economy and production that transitions from skirmish to large engagements.
- Distinct unit roles that reward composition and positioning.
- Strong strategic map control through expansion and denial.

## Product Principles

- Inspiration, not imitation: preserve genre strengths while creating original art, lore, units, names, and faction identity.
- Readability first: players should understand threats and opportunities quickly.
- Decisions over micromanagement: APM should matter, but strategic choices should matter more.
- Momentum with comeback paths: strong snowball potential balanced by recoverable states.

## Vertical Slice Definition

The first slice proves the identity through one playable match scenario with:

- Two original prototype factions with asymmetric strengths.
- Early expansion pressure and mid-game combined-arms interaction.
- A clear win and loss loop with readable UI feedback.
- Replay-ready scenario with repeatable testing flows.

## Out of Scope for Initial Slice

- Final narrative campaign.
- Full multiplayer stack.
- Final art and audio polish.

## Success Signals

- Functional: Core match loop can be completed repeatedly without blockers.
- Integration: Economy, production, movement, combat, and UI state stay coherent through a full match.
- Smoke: Build starts, match loads, and reaches end-state on target hardware.
- Observability: Match events and failures are diagnosable from logs and scenario notes.
